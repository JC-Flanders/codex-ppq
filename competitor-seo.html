FAQ

Before the first run.

What does this packet return?

It can return keyword gaps, competitor pages, ad themes, landing-page patterns, stack signals, screenshots, account notes, confidence labels, and open questions.

Which tools matter most?

SpyFu is the main competitive-intelligence source. BuiltWith, Apollo, Company Enrichment, and Diffbot add technology, account, identity, and page-structure context.

How much does a focused run cost?

The local model estimates about $0.52-$0.54 for a keyword-gap snapshot, $0.57-$0.58 for a paid-search audit, and $1.39-$1.51 for a broader launch brief.

Can the agent contact leads automatically?

No. Apollo records and account notes are candidates for human review. Outreach, signups, uploads, purchases, wallet actions, and mutations require explicit approval.

What should I watch for?

Domain confusion, stale ad data, geotargeted pages, bot-blocked pages, and dynamic costs. Keep timestamps, source links, and screenshots attached to the claims they support.
